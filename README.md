### Edd Clandestino

Founder and full-stack developer based in Puerto Rico. I build software for music, AI, and consumer marketplaces.

#### What I'm building

**HAOT** ([haot.app](https://haot.app))
A marketplace for in-person plans. Anyone hosts an activity, others join as a group. Matches by vibe, not proximity. Trust is earned by attendance. iOS and Android apps in active development, App Store launch Q3 2026.

**Clandestino Ventures and Ataraxia**
Music-industry AI studio. Three products shipped to date: transcription, A&R analytics, and sample-interpolation forensics. Open-sourced Ed25519-signed Trust Proofs.

#### Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Postgres, Supabase, Prisma, Stripe Connect, PostGIS. Solo full-stack from schema to production.

#### Background

Eight years in the music industry before going full-stack. Most recently Artist Relations PM and Label Manager at Rimas Entertainment / Sonar Music, working with CrisMJ, Hades66, Slayter, and Ankhal. Earlier roles at Defected Records (London) and Exhale Music (Belgium).
